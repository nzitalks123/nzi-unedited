Welcome to Nzi, Unedited 🎀

I don't really know how to start this.

Which is probably fitting, because I don't really know how most things are supposed to start.

I guess I'll start with this:

hi.

Welcome to Nzi, Unedited.

I've wanted to have a little space of my own for a while — somewhere I could write about whatever is on my mind without needing everything to fit into one neat little category.

So that's what this is.

Sometimes I'll talk about music. Sometimes school. Sometimes friendships, growing up, random things that happened during my day, things I'm learning about myself, or thoughts that decided to appear in my head at 1 a.m. for absolutely no reason.

There might be serious posts.

There might be completely unserious posts.

There will probably be playlists.

There will definitely be overthinking.

And honestly? I'm okay with that.

I don't want this blog to feel like I'm pretending to have everything figured out. I'm still growing, still changing my mind, still discovering what I like and who I want to become.

Maybe that's what “unedited” means to me.

Not perfect.

Not carefully planned.

Just real.

So, if you've somehow found your way here, welcome.

Stay for a post or two. Read something random. Find a song you like. Come back when you feel like it.

This is my little corner of the internet.

And we're just getting started. ♡

— nzi
